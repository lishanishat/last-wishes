# FTC Funeral Rule — consumer guidance excerpts (REAL public source).
# Checked against consumer.ftc.gov on 2026-11-20 (pages dated July 2012).
# Direct quotes are in quotation marks and under 15 words; the rest is close paraphrase.
# Format: entries separated by a line "---"; fields ID, TOPIC, URL, TEXT.

---
ID: FTC-1
TOPIC: alternative container for cremation
URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/ftc-funeral-rule
TEXT: A funeral home that offers cremations must tell you that alternative containers are available, and must make them available. They may be made of unfinished wood, pressed wood, fiberboard, or cardboard. "No state or local law requires the use of a casket for cremation."
---
ID: FTC-2
TOPIC: itemized prices / General Price List
URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/ftc-funeral-rule
TEXT: You can "get a written, itemized price list when you visit a funeral home," and funeral directors must give price information by telephone if you ask. After you decide what you want, and before you pay, you get a written statement listing every good and service selected, the price of each, and the total cost.
---
ID: FTC-3
TOPIC: choose only what you want / stated requirements
URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/ftc-funeral-rule
TEXT: "Buy only the funeral arrangements you want." You have the right to buy separate goods and services rather than a package. The written statement from the funeral home must explain any legal, cemetery, or crematory requirement that makes you buy a particular good or service.
---
ID: FTC-4
TOPIC: packages and embalming
URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/ftc-funeral-rule
TEXT: "You do not have to accept a package that may include items you do not want." "No state law requires routine embalming for every death." Services like direct cremation and immediate burial do not require any form of preservation.
