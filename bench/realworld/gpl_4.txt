General Price List
(Effective 7/3/2026)Thank you for choosing Price Funeral Chapel. We appreciate the trust you extend to our family as we serve your family. Our goal is to provide personalized meaningful service to you. At Price Funeral Chapel we are pleased to offer two methods by which services may be selected. You may either (1) choose from our itemized price list selecting only those services desired, or (2) choose one of our packaged service options that provide you convenience and significant cost savings. Should you choose traditional burial as the method of disposition, our service packages are available only to those persons who purchase the casket from Price Funeral Chapel. 
Prior to drafting any contract for goods or services, the responsible party or the decedent's survivor who is handling the funeral arrangements is entitled to receive a copy of any preneed agreement in the possession of the funeral establishment that has been signed and paid for, in full or in part, by or on behalf of the decedent. PRE-ARRANGED FUNERAL PLANS: We offer families pre-arranged and pre-paid funeral plans. If you pre-arrange through us, we will have all of your documentation on file. If you have a pre-paid funeral policy purchased through another mortuary, bring your documentation, as we will want to honor that plan. We encourage pre-payment of funeral expenses which offers the benefit of our full price guarantee, regardless of inflation. There are no fees for this service and affordable payments may be made over as many as 10 years. This provides peace of mind to not only you but to your family who would otherwise be financially responsible. Please feel free to discuss the details with us.
PAYMENT POLICY:Payment is due prior to the date of service or disposition. We accept one, or a combination, of the following: A) payment by cash, check, or credit card; or B) assignment of insurance policy benefits to Price Funeral Chapel with a fee of $400 or 7% of assigned value, whichever is greater.
OUT OF TOWN FAMILY:We can prepare letters for family members to use for bereavement benefits with airlines and employers.
DEATH CERTIFICATES:The County in which the death occurs produces certified copies of the death certificate. Certified copies may be needed for Social Security, Veteran’s benefits, insurance, title changes, trusts, banking, credit cards, stocks, bonds, etc. Anticipate approximately a 14-daywaiting period before copies become available from the County. We will call the family as soon as their documents are available.
VETERANS:Please provide the military discharge paper (Form DD 214) for a veteran. This document is necessary for veteran benefits such as a flag,grave marker, military honors, interment in a national cemetery, etc. We can assist with all these details.
OBITUARY:We can assist the family in preparing a basic obituary, including a photograph, for our website or any local and out of town newspaper.Newspapers charge per line, so the length of the obituary determines their cost.
VIDEOS/SLIDESHOWS:If the family desires, Price Funeral Chapel can create a slideshow to be played as part of services. On the other hand, if the family desires, the family may create a slideshow themselves. Price Funeral Chapel has the capability to play any slideshow or video created from your laptop by plugging the laptop directly into our flat screen television via an HDMI connection. We urge the family to bring your slideshow by our funeral home at least a day in advance to ensure that the slideshow is works.
