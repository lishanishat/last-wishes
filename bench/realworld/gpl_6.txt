Embalming and Professional Care......................................................................................$600
Embalming is a chemical process which provides temporary preservation of the body and eliminates certain health 
hazards. A person licensed by this state must complete this procedure.
Except in certain special cases, the law does not require embalming. Embalming may be necessary, however, if you select certain funeral 
arrangements, such as a funeral with public viewing. If you do not want embalming, you usually have the right to choose an arrangement 
that does not require you to pay for it, such as direct cremation or immediate burial.

Payment Policy 
To lose someone you love is a devastating experience. It is frequently traumatic and can seriously impact judgment. We believe in 
the importance of the recognition and meaningful celebration of a life that has been lived and will assist in arranging an appropriate 
service to meet your individual needs. 
It is also important to be realistic in respect to funeral expenditures. We do not want a family to spend more than they should nor 
less than they would like. What is important is that arrangements be in keeping with a family’s wishes and budget. 
Provisions for payment are due at time of arrangements. To avoid any possibility of misunderstanding or embarrassment and for 
your convenience, we accept the following methods of payment: 
Cash - Check - Debit Card -  ACH (e-check)
Credit Cards (2% fee will apply):  Visa  -  MasterCard  -  Discover  -  AmEx
Insurance Assignment of a verifiable policy ($150 processing fee)
Prepaid Arrangements
It is NOT the policy of Ohio Cremation & Memorial Society to bill an estate. In cases where the 
deceased’s estate must stand the burden of paying the funeral expenses, we ask that a family 
representative pay the funeral expenses and file claim for reimbursement with the deceased’s estate.
Cash Advance Items as Selected.
Third Party Cash Advance Items – Payment for cemetery, crematory, flowers, obituaries, death certificates, state sales 
tax, and honorariums of any type are required in the form of a check or cash before funeral services because each of these 
requires payment when their service is provided.
Unfortunately, since these are not our charges, we do not accept credit cards for these items.
Ohio Sales Tax applies to all merchandise and some services.
Obituaries: Prepared obituaries will be faxed or emailed by Ohio Cremation & Memorial Society to all newspapers requested by the 
authorizing agent. Any charges by the newspaper must be covered by a credit card at the time the obit is sent to the newspaper. 
Ohio Cremation & Memorial Society does not author obituaries and is not responsible for content or editing by newspaper. 
Certified Copies of DEATH CERTIFICATES: Ohio Cremation & Memorial Society is responsible for filing a completed Death Certificate 
with the Health Department in the county where the death occurred. We depend upon the United States Postal Service to deliver the 
death certificate to the doctor, back to us, and then to the Health Department. The family may order certified copies from the Health 
Department directly via fax, internet, phone or mail. This process takes a minimum of 2 weeks. If you wish for us to attempt to expedite 
the process there will be an additional charge with no guarantee of time savings. 
Personal Effects on the deceased at the time of death, or personal effects placed on the deceased for viewing purposes will be buried 
or cremated with the deceased unless specifically requested to be returned. If you would like the personal effects of the deceased 
from the time of death (such as the clothing, bedding, or jewelry) please ask for them, if not, we reserve the right to dispose of them 
after a 30-day period.
Ohio Cremation & Memorial Society rese rves the right to bury any unclaimed cremated remains (defined as: any cremated remains still 
in the custody of the funeral home 365 days following the cremation) in a common grave.
