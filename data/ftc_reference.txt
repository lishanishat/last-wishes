ID: FTC-1
TOPIC: alternative container for direct cremation
URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/ftc-funeral-rule
TEXT: Under the Funeral Rule, a funeral provider offering direct cremation must make an alternative container available, such as unfinished wood, pressed wood, fiberboard, or cardboard. A provider may not tell you that state or local law requires a casket for direct cremation, because none do.
---
ID: FTC-2
TOPIC: itemized prices / General Price List
URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/ftc-funeral-rule
TEXT: When you ask about arrangements in person, the funeral home must give you a written, itemized General Price List to keep. You also have the right to get price information over the phone. After you choose, the provider must give you an itemized statement of the goods and services selected, with prices and a total.
---
ID: FTC-3
TOPIC: choose only the goods and services you want / no false "required" claims
URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/ftc-funeral-rule
TEXT: You have the right to choose only the funeral goods and services you want, with limited exceptions such as the basic services fee. If the provider says a law, cemetery, or crematory requires you to buy a particular item, it must disclose that requirement in writing on the itemized statement and identify the specific law or rule.
---
ID: FTC-4
TOPIC: packages vs. itemized prices; embalming not generally required
URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/shopping-funeral-services
TEXT: Funeral homes may offer packages, but you can still buy individual items instead, and a package may include things you do not want. Embalming generally is not required by law, especially when a body is cremated promptly, and you can choose direct cremation, which has no viewing or visitation.
