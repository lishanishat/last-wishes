Death Certiﬁcates are oﬃcial documents issued by government authorities that record the details of a person's death. They are used for legal
proof of death, settling estates, insurance claims, pension and social security, healthcare records, closing accounts and services, and for
genealogy and historical records. Our $50 fee covers our staﬀ's time and eﬀort in obtaining and sending your certiﬁcates and is charged once,
no matter how many certiﬁcates you order. The state or county issuing the certiﬁcates sets its own per-certiﬁcate fee, which is billed in addition
to our fee and scales with the number of certiﬁcates requested.
